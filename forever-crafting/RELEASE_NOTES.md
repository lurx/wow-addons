Beta build of Forever Crafting for WoW Forever.

**Install:** download `ForeverCrafting-<version>.zip` below and unzip it. Put the `ForeverCrafting` folder and the profession folders you want into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`. Then type `/fc`.

**First beta (0.9.0)**
- Guides for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring.
- Gathering guides for Herbalism, Mining and Skinning, with camping recipes.
- Craft bar: one click opens the profession, selects the recipe and sets the amount the step needs.
- Nearest trainer with sub-area, coordinates and a map waypoint (TomTom and OpenMap supported).
- Bank counts in the guide and on the craft bar, and what to keep for later steps.
- Leftovers tab with vendor, auction and disenchant values.
- Experimental "Best value" hint, opt-in in Settings.

**Known gaps**
- Blacksmithing and Engineering follow Wowhead's route; a cheapest route isn't computed yet.
- Mining doesn't cover smelting recipes yet.
- English only.
